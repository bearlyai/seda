# Code of conduct

Seda is committed to a respectful, harassment-free project.

Be constructive, assume good intent while discussing ideas, respect personal
boundaries, and critique work rather than people. Harassment, threats,
discrimination, doxxing, or sustained disruption are not acceptable.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and restrict participation when
behavior harms the community. Report conduct concerns privately to
conduct@bearly.ai. Reports will be handled with discretion.
